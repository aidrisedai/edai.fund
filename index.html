01Why we’re doing this

“As a child, I used to fan the charcoal fire we cooked with at home. The charcoal would burn anyway, but you fanned it until it glowed with blue fire.”

Azeez Idris, PhD · Co-Founder and CEO, EdAI

Teens are the same. They have an idea, build something, maybe show it to someone. But few get the air they need to reach their full potential. They glow, but never at their brightest.

EdAI has always been about building a place where they can: where teens’ ideas are taken seriously, challenged honestly, and built side by side with real founders. The EdAI Fund puts real resources behind that promise. We can’t keep telling teens they have potential without backing them.

  1. i.

    Ambitious

    Ambition is the only way we know great work gets done. We back teens who want to build products people pay for, and who aim to do a lot of good in the world.

  2. ii.

    Principled

    Honesty, fairness and responsibility, guided by Islamic values and principles. We hold to them always, not just when it’s convenient. We build for now and for eternity.

  3. iii.

    Real, right now

    No toy projects. Teens work next to founders who share what they’ve learned and push for more. The goal is real-world value, now, not later.

EdAI is, and will always be, open to everyone.
