01

Matching our words with action

Why the fund belongs in the plan.

“As a child, I used to fan the charcoal fire we cooked with at home. The charcoal would burn anyway, but you fanned it until it glowed with blue fire.”

Azeez Idris, PhD · Co-Founder and CEO, EdAI

Teens are the same. They have an idea, build something, maybe show it to someone. Few get the air they need to glow at their brightest. We can’t keep telling teens they have potential without putting real resources behind them.

The final piece

Summits start relationships. Founder Clubs and Boardroom keep communities going. Cohorts and the AI Startup Coach build knowledge. Mentors help students decide. The fund would be the final piece: money for a venture when it has a real opportunity.

Most investors meet a founder once or twice. We would know founders over months or years, watching how they learn from users and handle setbacks. We think that could lead to better decisions, but it is an idea we still have to test.

Joining an EdAI program does not mean you will get investment.

EdAI Fund · Our valuesGuided by Islamic values
02

Ambition with a purpose

Islamic values in everyday building.

Islamic values give ambition a purpose and set rules for how we chase it. A young person’s talents are a trust, to develop and use well. EdAI is for anyone, whatever their faith or background. What never changes: we are always guided by Islamic values and principles.

Who are you helping?

//001

Whose life do you want to make better, and why does it matter? We back founders who can explain the good they do in words anyone would understand.

Say what you really did

//002

A demo is a prototype, not a finished product. A user is not a customer until they pay. Honesty means keeping promises and being clear about what your product can’t do yet.

Better, not perfect

//003

Ihsan means doing things with excellence and care. The first version can be small, but it should be made with care, and keep getting better.

Earning the right way

//004

Deal fairly, make clear promises, and take seriously the responsibility that comes with being paid. Profit matters. So does how you earn it.
